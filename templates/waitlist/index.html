Customers message at midnight, on four different apps, asking the same five questions. If you don't reply fast, they buy from whoever does. Closa makes sure that's you.

How it works

  1. Tell it about your business. Paste your website or upload your price list and catalog. Add your rules: delivery fees, discounts you allow, when you're open.
  2. Connect where you sell. WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, email and a chat box on your website. Takes a few minutes.
  3. Let it reply. It answers questions, sends payment links, books appointments and follows up. If it's not sure about something, it tells the customer you'll reply and pings you.

What you get

  • Replies in seconds, any time of day.
  • Payment links through Paystack or Stripe, right inside the chat.
  • Follow-ups for people who asked and disappeared.
  • One inbox for every app, so you can jump in whenever you want.
  • It only talks about your business. No making up prices or promises.
  • English, Pidgin, French and whatever tone fits your brand.

Why I'm building this

Most small businesses I know don't have a sales problem. They have a replying problem. The customers are already in the DMs asking to buy, but the owner is one person running the shop, the deliveries and the Instagram page at the same time.

I'm building Closa so that every message gets a proper answer, even at 2am, without hiring anyone. I'm starting with a small group of businesses and setting each one up myself.

Questions people ask

What if it tells a customer something wrong?

It only answers from what you give it. If a customer asks something it doesn't know, it says you'll get back to them and sends you a message. For the first week you can also approve replies before they go out.

Do I need WhatsApp Business?

Yes. We connect through Meta's official WhatsApp Business Platform, so your number stays safe. I'll help you set it up.

Can I still reply myself?

Any time. All your chats are in one place and you can take over a conversation with one tap.

Who is it for?

Anyone who sells through chat. Fashion, food, beauty, online stores, real estate, clinics, schools, services.
